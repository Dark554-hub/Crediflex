# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Crediflex is used by a personal lender in Merida, Yucatan, mainly while collecting payments in the field from a mobile phone. The day-to-day user needs to find a client quickly, choose the correct active credit, record payments, review balances, renew credits, and show or share receipts without fighting a desktop-style interface.

## Product Purpose

The system manages clients, credits, amortization schedules, daily and weekly collections, payments, receipts, settlement calculations, punctuality indicators, and a practical dashboard for a small lending operation. Success means the lender can run the route from an Android phone with clear numbers, fewer manual mistakes, and enough reporting to explain each client's status.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, direct. The app should feel like a field tool made for real collections: calm enough for financial work, fast enough for repeated use, and clear enough for non-technical users.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing pages, decorative dashboards, vague fintech gloss, tiny desktop tables on mobile, dense technical jargon, low-contrast gray text, and ad-like visual clutter. Do not make the interface feel like a generic SaaS template or a playful consumer app.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next collection action within one or two taps.
2. Make money, dates, payment status, and remaining balance visually impossible to miss.
3. Keep every form usable on a phone in the field, with large touch targets and predictable back paths.
4. Use plain Spanish labels that match how the lender talks about credits and payments.
5. Let settled or secondary information step back so active work stays in front.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA contrast, readable 16px+ form text on mobile, visible focus states, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and layouts that still work with larger browser text. The main user may be outdoors on a phone, so contrast and tap comfort matter more than decorative density.
